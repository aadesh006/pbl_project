Kernix

A lightweight kernel designed for low-level system control, interrupt-driven execution, and modular architecture suitable for embedded and resource-constrained environments. The system focuses on deterministic behavior, direct hardware interaction, and scalable kernel subsystem design.

Embedded & Critical Environment Relevance

Embedded deployments in critical environments require minimal latency, stable execution, and efficient hardware-level control. A compact kernel architecture enables faster boot time, reduced overhead, and predictable interrupt response, making such systems more reliable in constrained and unstable operational scenarios such as remote monitoring and autonomous devices.
